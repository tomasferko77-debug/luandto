About Lu&To

Lu&To represents the perfect harmony between two passionate artists who have found their musical soulmate in each other. Our singer brings years of classical training and a deep love for storytelling through song, while our pianist combines technical mastery with an intuitive understanding of melody and rhythm.

Together, we create intimate musical experiences that transport audiences through the emotional landscapes of chanson française, the sophisticated improvisations of jazz, and the heartfelt authenticity of folk traditions. Every performance is a conversation between voice and piano, creating moments of pure musical poetry.

Our Musical Journey

From intimate café performances to festival stages, we bring the same passion and artistry to every venue, creating unforgettable musical experiences for our audiences.

Our Music
